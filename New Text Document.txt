What can actually contribute?
A modern phone contains more sensing capability than most people realize.
Not every phone has every sensor, so the app should automatically detect hardware and enable only supported modules. Android and iPhone expose motion, orientation, location and environmental capabilities differently, while permissions also limit access to camera, microphone, nearby Wi-Fi and location.

Sensor fusion engine



The 30 meter map
The biggest design challenge is this:
A phone cannot magically know where every disturbance is located.
So Chiti Spectra should distinguish between measured, estimated, and unknown.
Every event receives one of three labels.
Classification	Meaning
🟢 Measured	Directly observed by hardware
🟠 Estimated	Position inferred from repeated movement and signal changes
🔴 Unknown origin	A real anomaly occurred but cannot be spatially located confidently
This honesty is important because it separates engineering from fiction.

How do we estimate location?
This is where the engineering becomes interesting.
A single phone standing still cannot accurately place every signal on a 30-meter map.
So Spectra builds the map while the user walks.
The algorithm uses repeated observations.
For example:
- At position A Wi-Fi signal = -48 dBm
- Walk 5 meters east
- Signal becomes -62 dBm
- Walk north
- Signal becomes -55 dBm
After enough measurements the app estimates a probable source region.
This is similar in spirit to radio mapping, not object detection.
Confidence increases gradually.

Disturbance score
Never create a fake “ghost probability.”
Instead calculate an Environmental Disturbance Index.
Formula concept:
\[
D=w_mM+w_aA+w_rR+w_vV+w_lL+w_nN
\]

Where each value is normalized environmental variation, not danger.
The UI explains why the score changed.
Example:
Disturbance 78/100

- Magnetic spike detected
- Three sharp acoustic impulses
- Bluetooth density increased
- Ambient light dropped suddenly
This is interpretable AI.
